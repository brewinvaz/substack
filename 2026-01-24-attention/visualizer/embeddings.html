Why This Matters

During training, the model learns that words appearing in similar contexts should have similar vectors. "The ___ sat on the mat" works with cat, dog, or child, so these words get pushed toward similar regions. Meanwhile, "algorithm" appears in completely different contexts, so it points in a very different direction.
