How It Works

The flywheel creates compounding advantages through feedback loops. Where does the learning accumulate? Two paths: train a selection model around a frozen API, or fine-tune your own model (SLM/open weights) directly. Either way, outcome data from deployment becomes your moat. See both approaches →
