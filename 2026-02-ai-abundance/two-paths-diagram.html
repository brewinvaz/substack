The Common Thread

Both paths follow the same flywheel logic: deploy, measure, learn, iterate. The difference is where the learning accumulates. With orchestration, your selector gets smarter at using a frozen model. With trainable models, the model weights themselves encode your domain knowledge. Many organizations start with orchestration to validate and collect data, then graduate to fine-tuned models once the economics justify the infrastructure investment.
